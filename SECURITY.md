# Security and Contact Policy

Edition label: `2026-10`

Security contact:

- Email: `security@parallax.example`
- Use this channel for disclosure of content integrity, publication safety, link-closure, or sensitive-data issues.

Scope:

- Public publication content only.
- Do not submit private corpus material here.

Response policy:

- Triage within the product owner workflow.
- Acknowledge receipt.
- Route corrections, removals, or safety concerns to the published corrections path.

Out of scope:

- Legal takedown demands that require human review outside the publication workflow.
- Private application incidents.
